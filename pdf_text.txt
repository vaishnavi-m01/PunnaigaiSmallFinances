Punnaigai Finances is a centralized financial management system designed to manage the complete
financial operations of a finance business.

2. PROJECT OBJECTIVES
The main objective of Punnaigai Finances is to create a transparent and centralized system for
managing money entering and leaving the business.
The system should:
Manage customer loans from application to completion.
Store customer documents and supporting proofs.
Track every customer payment.
Allow Agents to record customer collections.
Automatically identify overdue payments.
Apply configurable penalties.
Manage investments received from Investors.
Record payments made to Investors.
Maintain individual Investor wallets.
Manage Partner contributions.
Calculate and record Partner earnings.
Maintain individual Partner wallets.
Manage wallet withdrawals.
Record company income and expenses.
Maintain a complete transaction history.
Provide accurate financial reports.

35. IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULES
Rule 1: Company Fund and Individual Wallet Must Be Separate
Company money should not be directly confused with Investor or Partner wallet money.
Rule 2: Investment Amount and Wallet Balance Must Be Separate
Investment Amount ≠ Wallet Balance
Rule 3: Partner Contribution and Earnings Must Be Separate
Partner Contribution ≠ Partner Earnings ≠ Wallet Balance
Rule 4: Every Wallet Credit Must Have a Transaction
A wallet balance should never be manually increased without a transaction record.

Rule 5: Every Withdrawal Must Have a Transaction
A completed withdrawal must create a debit transaction.
Rule 6: Wallet Balance Cannot Become Negative
Users cannot withdraw more than their available balance.
Rule 7: Customer Documents Must Be Traceable
Every document must have an attachment and verification status.
Rule 8: Penalties Must Be Configurable
The Admin should configure penalty rules instead of hardcoding them.
Rule 9: Every Financial Activity Must Be Recorded
All money movement must create a transaction record.
